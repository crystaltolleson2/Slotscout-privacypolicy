Slot Scout Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Slot Scout account and associated personal data by contacting our support email below.

How to Request Deletion

Email crystaltolleson2@gmail.com with the subject line "Slot Scout Account Deletion."

Include the email address associated with your account. Do not send your password.

What Data Is Deleted?

We will delete your account and associated personal data, except information we are legally required to retain.

When Will Data Be Deleted?

Deletion requests will be processed within 30 days after verification.
